# Changelog

## 1.0.0

- First release. The house button adds a Paper Stage frame with a starter two-room floor plan and opens a 3D view of it.
- Rectangles, circles, and diamonds inside the frame stand up as walls. Stickies, doodles, text, images, lines, and arrows stand up as paper cutouts that turn to face you.
- Walk with WASD or the arrow keys and drag to look. Walls block you.
- Everyone on the board who opens the stage walks in the 3D view as a named paper figure and shows on the board as a named dot. Board cursors show in the 3D view as giant named hands.
- Edits inside the frame update the 3D view live for everyone on the stage.
